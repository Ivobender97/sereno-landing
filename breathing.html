Resting breathing rate counter

Count your dog's or cat's breaths while they sleep. Tap once for each breath. It's silent, works on your phone and needs no sign-up.

The timer starts with the first tap

How to count

  • Count while your pet is asleep or resting quietly, not right after play or a walk.
  • One breath is one rise and one fall of the chest.
  • With 30 seconds, the counter doubles the result for you.
  • What's normal for your pet, and how often to count, is something your vet will tell you.

Your last counts

    Saved only on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Keep every count, and see the trend

    In the Sereno app the same silent counter saves each count with a note, draws the trend over the weeks, tells you when resting breaths have been going up, and puts it all in a dated report for your vet, next to medications and dose changes.
