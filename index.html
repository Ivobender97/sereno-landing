Everything chronic-pet care actually needs

Powerful where it matters, calm everywhere else.

Insights

Sereno reads the data you've already entered and shows you the patterns in it: what's above or below your pet's usual range, what tends to happen at certain times of day, and what has changed since the last vet visit.

This is a pattern in the data you entered. It is not a diagnosis or a cause, and does not indicate any disease. Discuss your pet's health with your veterinarian.
