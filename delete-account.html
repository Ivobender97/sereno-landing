Delete Your Sereno Account

Applies to the app Sereno — Pet Health Tracker by Sereno.

In short

You can delete your Sereno account and all of its data at any time, directly in the app or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Sereno app and sign in.
  2. Tap the More tab (bottom-right).
  3. Open Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all associated data are permanently deleted right away.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer access the app, send an email to supportserenopet@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify your request and delete your account within 30 days.

What data is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes all of your data, including:

  • Your account and profile (email, display name)
  • All pets and their profiles
  • Vaccinations, vet visits, symptoms, medications and dose logs
  • Uploaded documents and photos
  • Family sharing memberships and invites
  • Notification and device registration data

Data retention

Your active data is deleted immediately upon request. Residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 30 days. We retain data longer only where required to comply with a legal obligation; in that case it is isolated and not used for any other purpose.

Questions

Contact us at supportserenopet@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
